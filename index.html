📌 General

What is the Coordinator Resources Centre?

The Coordinator Resources Centre is your central access point for ICT Programme dashboards, Survey123 forms, guides, QR codes, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and operational resources used to monitor and support programme implementation.

What can I access from this portal?

You can access:

  • ICT Programme Dashboards
  • Survey123 Forms
  • Coordinator Guides
  • QR Codes and URLs

📊 Dashboards

Which dashboards are available?

All dashboards developed in the programme will be added to the relevant StoryMap depending on who must access them. Additional dashboards will be published as the ICT Programme expands.

  • Facilitator Monitoring Dashboard
  • ICT Attendance Dashboard
  • ICT Helpdesk Dashboard

Why is my Facilitator Monitoring Dashboard not showing today's data?

The dashboard is configured to show today’s records. To see previous records, use the calendar filter.

  • Facilitators may not yet have submitted their forms.
  • Refresh the dashboard or browser page.
  • Check whether date or district filters are applied.

Refresh the dashboard before reporting a problem.

  • Scan the QR code using the Survey123 app.

Do facilitators need internet?

Yes. An internet connection is required to submit forms. When working offline, submissions will sync once connectivity is restored.

🕒 Clock In / Clock Out

What if a facilitator forgets to clock in?

The clock-in system has compliance standards that must be enforced for the success of the programme. Facilitators are allowed to clock in during the permitted window from 06:00 to 08:00. After 08:00, the system will still allow clock-in, but the facilitator will be flagged as late and prompted to provide a reason. After 12:00, the system closes and no clock-in is allowed.

The coordinator should follow the ICT Programme SOP for missed clock-ins and record the reasons where required.

What if a facilitator forgets to clock out?

The clock-out system has compliance standards that must be enforced for the success of the programme. Facilitators are allowed to clock out during the permitted window from 12:00 to 16:00. After 16:00, the system will still allow clock-out, but the facilitator will be flagged as late and prompted to provide a reason when working late. After 18:00, the system closes and opens again the next day.

Repeated missed or late clock-outs should be addressed according to programme procedures.

Why is the Venue Change form important?

The Venue Change form records movement between schools or programme locations, ensuring dashboards accurately reflect facilitator activities throughout the day.

🛠 ICT Helpdesk

When should an ICT Helpdesk Support Form be submitted?

Only after the ICT Champion or Committee Member has completed the ICT Pre-Check Checklist and the issue cannot be resolved locally.

Why is the Pre-Check Checklist important?

The checklist helps identify common issues that can often be resolved immediately, such as:

  • Equipment switched off
  • Loose cables
  • Internet connectivity
  • Display settings
  • User login issues

Completing the checklist first reduces unnecessary site visits and speeds up support for genuine faults.
